# ✨ Sparkle

A Dynamic Island–style notch for Windows 10 and 11. It sits at the top of your screen, stays out of the way, and opens when you need it.

### [⬇️ Download the latest version](https://github.com/bryanvegards/SPARKLE---WINDOWS-NOTCH/releases/latest)

On that page, click **SparkleSetup-….exe** under **Assets** (not "Source code").

## What it does
- 🎵 **Music:** album art, controls and a waveform for Spotify, YouTube and more, with **synced lyrics** that you can fix if they're off (wrong song or timing)
- ✅ **Tasks:** type in plain words ("gym 7-8pm tomorrow"), with a week/month calendar. Unfinished tasks move to today, and finished ones clear after a week
- 🎯 **Focus:** a pomodoro timer that lives in the notch
- 💧 **Water:** a daily goal with gentle reminders
- 📝 **Notes:** a quick scratch pad, or save notes to keep
- ⭐ **Little touches:** twinkling stars when idle, charging lights, smooth Apple-style animations

## Make it yours
Click the ⚙ in the top-right of the notch:
- **Open on click:** open the notch with a click instead of hovering
- **Minimal notch:** just a thin line when idle, and a tiny box with music or a timer
- **Synced lyrics**, **waveform animation**, **start with Windows**, water settings and more

## Installing
1. Download and run the installer.
2. If Windows says **"Windows protected your PC"**, click **More info → Run anyway** (the app isn't signed with a paid certificate).
3. Sparkle appears at the top of your screen.

**It updates itself.** Every update is signed and checked before it installs, and you can choose "ask first" in Settings.

Works on Windows 10 (1809+) and Windows 11, 64-bit. Your tasks, notes and settings stay on your PC.
